`book/src/gridspec/friends.md` § Not measured: what a friend granted the edit
right (`GrantUserRights` 4) may do with the granter's objects on each grid —
the update flags it is sent for one (modify, move, copy, `OBJECT_OWNER_MODIFY`),
which edits each grid takes from it and which it refuses, and what the owner
and the editor are told. A census in the shape of `object-edit-stranger`, with
the stranger made a friend holding the right.
